## Root-cause boundary

The normal projector validates a call-output pair and then sends its output
through the same strict input-content projector used by ordinary messages. An
exact empty string is consequently rejected before upstream I/O even when the
call kind and `call_id` relationship are complete.

This is a request-copy transformation. No repair requires or authorizes edits
to Codex JSONL, SQLite, transcript history, archives, resume pointers, or model
metadata. AIGW continues to own provider configuration, credentials, endpoint
selection, and provider switching.
