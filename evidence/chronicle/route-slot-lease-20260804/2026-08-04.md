# Route slot lease evidence — 2026-08-04

## Facts

- 2026-08-03: A Codex client on the `dmxapi` route received
  `unexpected status 503 Service Unavailable: local proxy overloaded: timed out
  waiting for responses concurrency slot (8)` against
  `http://127.0.0.1:8792/dmxapi/v1/responses`. The `(8)` named the process-wide
  limit; the binding constraint was the per-route single-flight slot introduced
  by `2026-08-03-route-scoped-backpressure`.
- 2026-08-03: Operational-log census over the local corpus counted 16836
  route-slot holds. Duration distribution: 0-30s 15889; 30-60s 696; 60-120s 211;
  120-240s 35; 240-300s 4; 300-600s 0; 600s and above 1. The single hold above
  600s ran 655s and ended `terminal=response.completed events=172`, so it was a
  legitimate long turn rather than a stall.
- 2026-08-03: 40 of 16836 holds exceeded the released 120-second
  `responses_queue_timeout` default and 1 exceeded 300 seconds. The observed
  denials are therefore explained by legitimate holds outlasting the operator's
  configured queue wait, not by a hung stream. `sse_pre_content_reconnect`
  appears once in the same corpus.
- 2026-08-03: Reading `transport/sse.py` at `74ca0eb` established that the relay
  armed only `upstream_read_timeout` per read and that `upstream_timeout`
  bounded `urlopen` alone, so one route-slot hold had no total wall-clock
  ceiling. Commit `60e15e1` names the route width and reports route, route
  limit, and process limit in the denial.
- 2026-08-04: Commit `680088a` threads one `time.monotonic() + UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT`
  deadline through every relay attempt, clamps each per-read budget to the
  remaining deadline, refuses reconnect past the deadline, and closes every
  upstream response the relay stops reading. The socket leak was confirmed as a
  `ResourceWarning` under `PYTHONWARNINGS=error` before the fix.
- 2026-08-04: Commit `dbfd8de` advertises `Retry-After: 5` on the queue-timeout
  503 and adds the diagnosis-table row. RED was observed first on both tests
  (`AssertionError: None != '5'` and `('Retry-After', '5') not found in

- 2026-08-04: Widening `RESPONSES_MAX_PER_ROUTE` above 1 was evaluated and
  rejected. `transport/cooldown.py` populates `_FAILURES` at one assignment,
  reached only through `remember_failure`, whose two production callers in
  `transport/exchange.py` both run after the exchange returns — the rate-limit
  path gated on `status_code == 429` and the non-429 wire-policy rejection. At
  width 2 both simultaneously admitted same-route requests therefore read
  `remaining() == 0` and both reach the provider. That is the exact failure
  `2026-08-03-route-scoped-backpressure` closed one day earlier. The post-queue
  cooldown recheck cannot compensate because it only protects a request that
  waited. A per-provider width knob is separately forbidden by the
  provider-manifest discipline in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
- 2026-08-04: The recurrence lever is the pre-existing operator knob
  `CODEX_RESPONSES_PROXY_RESPONSES_QUEUE_TIMEOUT`
  (`DEFAULT_RESPONSES_QUEUE_TIMEOUT = 120.0`, bounds 0.001-3600). Raising it to
  300 covers 39 of the 40 observed holds above 120 seconds with no code change
  and no loss of burst protection; a queued request holds no global slot because
  the route semaphore is acquired first. The 655-second hold stays a denial by
  design: 300 seconds bounds the queue wait, while the separate 900-second
  ceiling bounds the hold itself.
- 2026-08-04: Raising the released default in code was evaluated and rejected on

  `Environment=CODEX_RESPONSES_PROXY_RESPONSES_QUEUE_TIMEOUT=120.0` — and the
